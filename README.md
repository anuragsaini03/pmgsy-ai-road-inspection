# 🛣️ PMGSY AI Road Inspection & Defect Detection System

An AI-powered automated road inspection and maintenance monitoring system designed for the **Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY)** initiative. This platform uses computer vision and geospatial analytics to detect, classify, and map road defects—such as potholes and surface cracks—enabling faster, objective, and data-driven infrastructure auditing for rural roads across India.

## 🔍 Overview

Manual inspection of thousands of kilometers of rural roads built under the PMGSY scheme is labor-intensive, time-consuming, and subject to human bias. 

This project automates the quality auditing lifecycle:
1. **Field Collection:** Inspectors capture images or video feeds of roads with embedded GPS tags.
2. **AI Analysis:** Computer vision models scan media to localize and classify road damage severity.
3. **Geospatial Mapping:** Detected defects are plotted on interactive maps to track road degradation over time.
4. **Actionable Insights:** Automated reports help government engineers prioritize repair budgets and assign maintenance tasks efficiently.
